Author Contributions

algorithm, batch docking pipeline, and symmetric RMSD validation module, and conducted
the benchmark validation and data analysis. S.T. assisted with software development
and performed validation experiments. I.A.K. conceived the original concept for the
project and supervised the work. M.Z.A. supervised the project. All authors
contributed to writing and reviewing the manuscript and approved the final version.
