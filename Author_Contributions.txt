Author Contributions

B.S. designed and implemented the IBDock software, including the grid box derivation
algorithm, batch docking pipeline, and symmetric RMSD validation module. B.S. carried out
the large majority of the docking runs (Astex benchmark, cross-docking, repurposing, and the
aging-associated case study). S.T. performed the data analysis (pose-recovery scoring,
seed-consensus and statistical analyses, cross-docking and repurposing analyses) and
contributed to the docking runs. B.S. and S.T. jointly prepared the figures and the
supporting information. I.A.K. conceived the original concept for the project and
supervised the work. M.Z.A. supervised the project. All authors contributed to writing
and reviewing the manuscript and approved the final version.
